# Proposed €29 agency pilot — not available for purchase

## Check the files behind your WooCommerce downloads

For technical agencies maintaining at least two WooCommerce stores selling files hosted locally under WordPress uploads.

**Available now, free:** the local diagnostic in this repository. It checks supplied file records for missing files, zero-byte files and empty inventories. Start with the synthetic example; no shop login is needed to try it.

**Proposed price: €29 for the first month, up to five compatible stores, excluding applicable indirect taxes.** Intended month-to-month price after activation: €29/month. Proposed scope: weekly inventory checks, change-only reports, missed-run warnings and a monthly summary under your agency name.

Those recurring functions are **not built or operating**. There is no current checkout, order, subscription or delivery commitment. Any later paid pilot/preorder would need separately approved terms, a stated delivery date and refund conditions before payment. Do not send money, payment details or credentials through GitHub.

### Scope and exclusions

- Local uploads files only; no external/CDN/S3/signed URL monitoring.
- Not an end-to-end purchase or buyer-download test.
- No repairs, custom integrations, emergency response or live shop access.
- The free utility requires a complete authorised export and matching local files; it does not collect products or schedule itself.
- Nothing is transmitted by the diagnostic. GitHub issue replies, however, are public.
